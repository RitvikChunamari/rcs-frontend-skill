# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation should be welcoming, constructive, and focused on improving safe, accessible frontend work.

Positive participation includes specific evidence-based feedback, respect for different experience levels and design perspectives, proper source credit, and responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or submitting stolen, unlicensed, or malicious material is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact options. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
