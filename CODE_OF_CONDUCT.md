# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, constructive, and focused on improving safe, accessible frontend work.

Examples of positive participation include:

- Giving specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels, identities, tools, and design perspectives.
- Crediting sources and honoring licenses.
- Accepting corrections and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or submitting stolen, unlicensed, or malicious material.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact options. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
