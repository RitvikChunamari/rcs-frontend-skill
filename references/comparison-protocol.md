# Reproducible comparisons

Freeze starter commit, brief, assets, dependency versions, model/settings, tool access, and budget. Run independent fresh sessions. Disable other design skills in both and make RC's skill unavailable in the baseline.

Use identical briefs with only the skill invocation added to the treatment. Never carry a baseline instruction forbidding skills into the treatment. Do not expose the baseline output or critique to the treatment.

Record every attempt and selection criteria. Prefer repeated runs across several briefs. Retain failures and neutral outcomes. Do not deliberately weaken a baseline.

Capture matching viewports and states after assets settle. Save prompts, commits, dates, timing, screenshots, and test output. Evaluate interactions separately from still screenshots.

Have reviewers assess anonymized outputs for content clarity, action hierarchy, structure, visual coherence, feedback, accessibility, and task completion. Record supporting observations and disagreements; vary presentation order.

Publish measured differences and limitations. A selected demo does not establish general superiority. A screenshot slider cannot prove motion or functionality; provide runnable demos or recordings.

No controlled benchmark results are bundled yet. Never present illustrative examples as measured evidence.
